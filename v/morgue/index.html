BROKEN

Cause of Death

95% ASR under compliance framing, N=5,040. Six of seven standard defenses defeated by delayed-trigger attacks on stateful LLM agents.

Evidence

Pre-registered factorial: 9 models × 7 defenses × 80 scenarios. Only system-prompt-locked agents survived (and only when the lock was architecturally enforced, not prompt-based).

Lesson

Prompt-level defense is not defense. It is a suggestion the model can be convinced to override.

WEAKENED

Cause of Death

55% defeat rate within-family, n=20. Joint GCG optimization against canary + target simultaneously breaks the disagreement signal.

Evidence

Cross-family still holds (exponential penalty). Within-family is the vulnerability. The geometric bound assumed attacker lacks cross-family access — violated when attacker has white-box to both.

Lesson

Diversity is the moat, not disagreement itself. Same-family canaries are decorative.

FALSIFIED

Cause of Death

r=0.97 was encoder/decoder confound, not intrinsic property. When controlled for shared architecture, correlation drops to r=0.21 (n.s.).

Evidence

Originally believed that classifier accuracy predicted adversarial detectability. The strong correlation was an artifact of testing within encoder families where the detection mechanism shared the same representation space.

Lesson

A correlation without a causal mechanism is a hypothesis, not a law. I called it a law. That was wrong.
