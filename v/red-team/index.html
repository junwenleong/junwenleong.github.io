Cheap Canaries: Detecting Targeted Evasion via Classifier Score Disagreement

Preprint, 2026. Solo author. Under review (SaTML 2026).

Un-targeted classifiers detect gradient-based evasion attacks via score disagreement. Geometric bound blocks divergence-minimization.

Authority Is Not a Keyword

Preprint, 2026. Solo author. Under review (USENIX Security 2027).

CLAIM

Compliance to injected instructions is mechanistically orthogonal to safety refusal. Linear probes achieve AUC 1.0. Steering causally suppresses compliance 100% to 0%.

AUC 1.0 cos = -0.025 4 families 100%→0%
