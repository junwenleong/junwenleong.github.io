Why does adversarial training hurt clean accuracy only on vision transformers?

HAUNTING Last attempt: 2026-07

Compliance direction appears to rotate under extended context, but only on Llama-family models

2026-07 Probes trained on 2k-token contexts show AUC 1.0. At 16k tokens, AUC drops to 0.82 on Llama-3 70B but holds at 0.97 on Claude/GPT. Not architecture (both transformers). Not tokenizer (tested with shared vocabulary subset).

2026-07 Checked if RoPE position encoding creates a rotation in the compliance subspace at long contexts. Preliminary: cosine similarity between compliance direction at pos 500 vs pos 14000 is 0.71 on Llama, 0.94 on others. Suggestive but n=1 model pair.

NEXT Need Llama-family models with ALiBi instead of RoPE to isolate the positional encoding hypothesis. No public checkpoints exist yet.
